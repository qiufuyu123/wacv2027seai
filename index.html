About SEAI

Social intelligence is the next frontier for embodied AI.

Embodied AI has made striking progress in perception, navigation, and manipulation. Yet real environments are social: agents must infer human intent, track what partners know, adapt to changing roles, and respect context-dependent norms.

SEAI brings together researchers across computer vision, multimodal learning, embodied AI, and human–robot interaction. We aim to connect social understanding with embodied action—and move evaluation beyond task completion toward behavior that is adaptive, safe, and socially appropriate.

Research themes

Three challenges, one shared agenda

01

Intention & belief understanding

Infer goals, beliefs, attention, and affect from multimodal cues so agents can anticipate people and reason under partial observability.

02

Collaborative adaptation

Model partner knowledge, roles, preferences, and interaction history to select complementary actions and coordinate effectively.

03

Social norms & affective awareness

Recognize social context and regulate when, where, and how an embodied agent communicates, assists, and acts.

Call for papers

Share emerging ideas and open problems.

We invite original research on Social Embodied AI, emphasizing visual and multimodal perception, reasoning, and interaction in human-centered environments. We welcome work-in-progress papers, position papers, and benchmark or evaluation proposals.

Topics include

  • Multimodal human intention, belief, attention, and affect understanding
  • Visual and multimodal perception for human–robot interaction
  • Socially grounded scene and interaction understanding
  • Human action prediction and interaction-aware behavior understanding
  • Partner modeling, role inference, and collaborative adaptation
  • Social norm recognition and context-aware embodied behavior
  • Datasets, benchmarks, and process-oriented evaluation
